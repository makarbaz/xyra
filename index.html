What is XYRA? +

XYRA is a local discovery app concept designed to help people find nearby stores, offers and useful places around them.

Does XYRA use my location? +

Location can help personalise nearby results. Features that rely on location should work only with the permissions you choose to grant.

Can a local business partner with XYRA? +

Local businesses can contact the XYRA team to discuss partnership and future offer-listing opportunities.
