THE IDEA

MADE FOR EVERYDAY DISCOVERY

There's more to a city than the places you already know.

Local discovery can be fragmented: a recommendation in one place, a discount in another and a great little shop you only notice after walking past it for months.

XYRA is being developed to bring that experience closer together. The aim is to help people explore nearby stores and local offers while giving neighbourhood businesses another path to discovery.

It is not about making people travel further. It is about helping them see more of where they already are.
