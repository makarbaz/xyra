01 / THE BASICS

About XYRA

What is XYRA? +

XYRA is a local discovery app in development, designed to help people explore stores, services and offers around a current or selected location.

Is XYRA available everywhere? +

Coverage will depend on the product launch and participating businesses. The website does not claim live offer coverage in every city.

Is the app live today? +

This website introduces XYRA and its intended experience. Use the contact page to express interest in early access and follow product updates.

02 / YOUR CHOICE

Location & alerts

Does XYRA need my location? +

Location can help show relevant nearby stores and offers. The intended experience also supports manual area selection. Location-dependent features should only use permissions you grant.

Will XYRA send alerts when I'm near a store? +

Location-aware nearby offer alerts are part of the intended product experience. Actual notification behaviour will depend on the release version, device permissions and available offers.

Can I use XYRA without background location access? +

Manual or in-app discovery can be offered without background location access. Alerts that rely on detecting location while an app is closed may not be available without the required permissions.

03 / FIND SOMETHING GOOD

Local offers

Are offers guaranteed to be live? +

Offers will depend on participating businesses and the details they provide. Before using any offer, check its terms, validity dates and store restrictions.

What does “offers near me” mean on XYRA? +

It describes the idea of discovering local deals and stores around a current or selected location rather than searching for every place manually.

Can I save offers or stores? +

Saving favourite offers and stores is part of the experience being developed. Exact features may depend on the released version.

04 / LOCAL PARTNERS

For businesses

Can I register my shop with XYRA? +

The business listing and offer-management service is still being developed. Contact XYRA with your store name, area and goals to express interest.

How much does a business partnership cost? +

Pricing and partnership terms have not been announced. XYRA will share details when the business programme is ready.

Will XYRA guarantee more sales? +

No sales or footfall results are guaranteed. The goal is to develop a more useful way for nearby customers to discover local businesses.
